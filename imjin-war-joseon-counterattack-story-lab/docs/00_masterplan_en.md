# Imjin War Early Land Scenario Game Masterplan

Date: 2026-05-01

The project is now a playable local fanfic game focused on the opening phase of the Imjin War. Admiral Yi Sun-sin must remain alive. The game begins with Busanjin and Dongnae in April 1592, and the main gameplay is land defense, evacuation, dispatch delivery, beacon protection, and supply survival.

## Working Title

`Joseon's Counterattack: Dawn of Dongnae Fortress`

## Fixed Rules

- Yi Sun-sin is alive.
- The story starts at the beginning of the war.
- The playable combat focus is land warfare, not naval warfare.
- Visuals are original canvas-drawn fan assets inspired by the public concept of `Imjin War: Joseon's Counterattack`.
- No original game files, official images, UI, voices, or extracted assets are used.

## Playable Structure

1. Busanjin Dispatch
2. The Gate of Dongnae
3. Seeds of Counterattack

## Main Character

Han Do-yun is a fictional low-ranking arsenal artisan attached to Dongnae. He handles powder, timber, signal fires, civilians, and emergency weapons rather than fighting as a famous commander.
